Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 22, 2026
1. Who we are
Technologie Intelligente Mondiale Inc. (“AVANTPART,” “the Company,” “we,” “us”) is a Canadian company based in Quebec that operates the AVANTPART brand and the website https://avantpart.com.
This website is an online store for customers in the United States. You can also contact us through live chat or email for help with your purchase or product. Our products are sold mainly through avantpart.com and Amazon.com, and may also be sold through Amazon.ca.
This policy explains what personal information we may collect, why, who may handle it, and what choices and rights you have. Third parties such as Amazon, PayPal, tawk.to, Google, Microsoft and Meta handle information under their own privacy policies.
2. Product support and live chat
When you contact us for support by live chat or email, we use your information to answer your request and help with your product. We do not use support requests or information received from Amazon for marketing, and we handle information received from Amazon as Amazon’s policies require.
Live chat is provided by tawk.to and is available on our pages. It loads only if you accept optional cookies. If you decline, you can still reach us by email at info@avantpart.com.
Please do not send payment card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information by chat or email.
3. Information we may collect
• Support: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and the content of your messages.
• Website orders: your name, contact details, shipping address, the products ordered, and order and payment confirmation. Payments are handled by PayPal, and we do not receive or store your full card number.
• Amazon: limited order or support information that Amazon makes available to us under its policies.
• Technical information: such as your IP address, browser, device, pages visited and access times, processed by our website and technical providers, and by the cookie tools described in Section 6 if you accept them.
4. How we use information
We use personal information to provide support, process website orders and returns, keep the website secure, prevent fraud, keep required records and meet legal obligations. If you accept optional cookies, we also use them to understand how the website is used and to measure advertising for our online store.
5. Sharing
We do not sell or rent the personal information you give us for support or orders. We may share information with providers that help us operate, such as payment, live chat, hosting, email, fulfilment and shipping providers, and, if you accept optional cookies, analytics and advertising measurement tools. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.
If you accept advertising cookies, Meta and Google receive information about your visit directly from your browser and may use it for their own purposes under their privacy policies. Under some U.S. state laws this may be treated as “sharing” for advertising. You can decline or withdraw as described in Section 6.
6. Cookies and similar technologies
When you first visit, you can choose Accept All Cookies or Reject Non-Essential Cookies. Cookies that are not strictly necessary are used only if you accept. They include:
• Necessary: keep the site working and remember your cookie choice.
• Chat: tawk.to.
• Analytics: tools such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.
• Advertising and measurement: tools such as Meta Pixel and Google Ads, used for our online store.
Embedded YouTube videos may cause YouTube or Google to process technical information such as your IP address when a video loads or plays.
You can change your choice at any time using Cookie Preferences on the website or your browser settings. The tools we use may change over time. Our website does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

8. Where information is stored
Some of our providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. For example, tawk.to states that its chat data is stored in the United States.
9. How long we keep information
We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy or as the law requires. For example, accounting and tax records are kept for the period that tax law requires. Providers may keep information under their own policies.
10. Security
We use reasonable measures to protect personal information and limit access to people who need it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
11. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you, give you access to it, correct it, delete it where the law allows, or provide it in a commonly used format, and you may withdraw a consent you gave us, including your cookie choices. To make a request, email info@avantpart.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within the time the law requires.
If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or another competent authority. U.S. residents may also have rights under their state’s privacy law, including to opt out of advertising-related sharing.
12. Children
Our website is for a general audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 without a parent’s or guardian’s consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us.
13. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
